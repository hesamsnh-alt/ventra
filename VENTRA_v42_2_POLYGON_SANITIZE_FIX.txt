Ventra v42.2 - Floor Polygon Sanitizer

Fixes THREE.Shape duplicate-point and polygon triangulation warnings during
2D -> 3D reconstruction.

Before ShapeGeometry each floor polygon now:
- converts points to finite numeric coordinates
- removes consecutive duplicate / near-duplicate points
- removes duplicated closing point
- removes near-collinear intermediate vertices
- rejects self-intersecting polygons
- rejects near-zero-area polygons
- validates that ShapeGeometry produced actual vertices

Invalid floor polygons are skipped instead of poisoning the reconstruction.
Wall reconstruction continues normally.

The reconstruction summary now reports skipped invalid floor polygons.
No re-analysis is required.
