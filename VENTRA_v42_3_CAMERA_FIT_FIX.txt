Ventra v42.3 - Reconstruction Camera Fit

Problem:
The 3D wall meshes were successfully built (232 verified wall segments) but the
Autodesk camera remained fitted to the underlying 2D sheet.

Fix:
- Calculates a THREE.Box3 bounding box from every reconstructed wall/floor mesh.
- Stores the reconstruction bounds with the reconstruction result.
- Immediately switches the Viewer to perspective mode.
- Positions the camera in an isometric-style view around the reconstruction
  bounding box.
- Uses Z as the camera up vector.
- Activates Orbit.
- Fit to View now targets the reconstructed 3D bounds when reconstruction exists.
- Home also returns to the reconstructed 3D view when reconstruction exists.
- Original Autodesk fitToView remains the fallback when there is no reconstruction.

No Analyze Drawing is required.
No OpenAI call.
