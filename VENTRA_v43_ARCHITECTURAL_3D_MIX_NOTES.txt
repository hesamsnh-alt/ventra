Ventra v43 - Architectural 3D Mix

Adds:
- existing verified wall reconstruction
- two-storey stacking
- source sheet removal
- door geometry markers from verified door dbIds / saved door segments
- window/glazing geometry markers from verified window dbIds / saved window segments
- one structural slab per detected storey
- slab is generated from the convex hull of the verified storey wall geometry
- door default height 2.10 m
- window default sill 0.90 m / height 1.20 m
- slab thickness 0.18 m
- all geometry uses the local metre coordinate system
- no OpenAI call
- no Analyze Drawing required when legacy dbIds/line geometry exist

Important honesty:
Door/window markers are verified by source geometry/dbId where available, but
v43 does NOT yet boolean-cut the reconstructed walls. They visually identify
opening locations. Actual wall void subtraction is the next geometry stage.

Slabs are provisional_convex_hull, not claimed to be measured floor polygons.
This avoids the corrupt raw floor topology that previously caused
THREE triangulation failures.
