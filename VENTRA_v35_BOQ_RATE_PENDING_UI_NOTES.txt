Ventra v35 - BOQ Measured / Rate Pending UI

Purpose:
Make verified APS quantities commercially safe and understandable in the BOQ UI.

Fixes:
- Preserves rateMissing and measuredFromApsStructure through normalizeItems().
- A measured_rate_pending row now shows:
  MEASURED / RATE PENDING
  and Amount displays RATE PENDING instead of AED 0.00.
- Adds a page warning explaining that verified geometry is excluded from the
  commercial subtotal until a rate/grade is selected.
- Commercial subtotal now obeys includeInCommercialTotal instead of including
  every verification='verified' row.
- Applying a UAE Price Library rate to a measured_rate_pending row promotes it
  to measured, clears rateMissing, and includes it in the commercial subtotal.
- Manually entering a positive rate does the same, with source Manual confirmed rate.
